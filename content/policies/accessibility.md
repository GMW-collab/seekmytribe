We want everyone to be able to find out about and take part in our activities,
including people who use assistive technology, people with limited internet
access and people who aren't confident online.

## This website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.
The site is designed to:

- work with keyboard only, with a clear visible focus outline
- work with screen readers, with proper headings, labels and landmarks
- use text and colour combinations with strong contrast
- respect your device setting for reduced motion
- work on small screens, slow connections and older devices
- use a typeface designed for readers with low vision

### Known limitations

- We have not yet tested the site with a wide range of disabled users. We plan to.
- Some documents we publish in future may be PDFs. We will always offer the same information in another format on request.

## Our activities

Every activity page lists what we know about access: step-free routes, toilets,
seating, noise levels, lighting and transport. When we haven't checked
something, we say so rather than guess.

Carers and support workers are welcome at no cost.

## Asking for something different
