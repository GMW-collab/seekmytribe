We want to hear when something hasn't worked, whether it's a small thing at an
activity or a bigger problem with how we've treated you.

## How to tell us

Email {{email}}, use our [contact form](/contact/), or speak to an organiser at
any activity. Tell us what happened and what you'd like us to do. You can ask
someone else to contact us on your behalf.

## What happens next

- We'll acknowledge your complaint within 5 working days.
- A director will look into it, and we'll give you a full response within 20 working days. If it will take longer, we'll tell you why.
- If you're not satisfied, you can ask for it to be reviewed by a different director.

We keep complaints confidential, and making one will never affect whether you
can take part in our activities.

## If it's about safety

Please see our [safeguarding page](/safeguarding/), which explains how to raise
a concern about someone's safety. If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.

## Beyond us

Complaints about how we handle personal information can go to the Information
Commissioner's Office. Concerns about whether a CIC is serving the community
can be raised with the Office of the Regulator of Community Interest Companies,
though it doesn't resolve individual disputes.
