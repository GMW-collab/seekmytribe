This notice explains how {{legal_name}} ("we") uses personal information
collected through this website. We are the controller of that information.
You can contact us about anything in this notice at {{email}}.

## What we collect and why

We only collect what you choose to send us through a form.

| Form | What we ask for | Why | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contact | Name, email, your message, and optionally a phone number | To reply to you | Legitimate interests |
| Coming to an activity | Name, email, which activity, and optionally a phone number and notes for the host | To let the host expect you and tell you about changes | Legitimate interests |
| Register interest | Name, email, area, interests | To tell you when something suitable starts | Consent |
| Suggest an activity | Your idea, area, and optionally your name and email | To plan activities and, if you ask, to reply | Legitimate interests |
| Volunteer and contribute | Name, email, how you'd like to help, availability | To discuss volunteering with you | Legitimate interests |
| Partners and funders | Name, organisation, email, your enquiry | To respond to your enquiry | Legitimate interests |
| Updates | Email, and optionally your name | To send occasional news | Consent |

We ask you not to share health or other sensitive information through the
website. If you need to tell us about an access requirement, we'll use that
only to make the adjustment you ask for.

## Who sees it

Form submissions are sent by email to the people who run Seek My Tribe. We use
these service providers, who process information on our behalf:

- **Email delivery:** {{email_provider}}
- **Website hosting:** {{hosting_provider}}

We don't sell or share your information for marketing, and we don't use it to
make automated decisions about you.

## Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. If we
ever add them, we will update this notice and ask for your consent first where
the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiries and volunteer or partner messages: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
- Activity sign-ups: shared with that activity's host, and deleted 3 months after the activity. We keep only anonymous attendance counts.
- Activity suggestions: the suggestion itself may be kept to help plan activities, but your name and email are deleted after 12 months.
- Register-interest and updates lists: until you ask us to stop, or after two years without contact.

## Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, to
stop using it, or to stop contacting you. Withdrawing consent is as easy as
replying "stop" or emailing {{email}}. We'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us
first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's
Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113.
