Everyone who comes to a Seek My Tribe activity should be safe and treated with
respect. We take any concern seriously, however small it seems.

## If someone is in immediate danger

Call **999**.

## Raising a concern

If you're worried about someone's safety or wellbeing, or about how someone has
behaved at one of our activities, contact our safeguarding lead:

- **Safeguarding lead:** {{safeguarding_lead}}
- **Email:** {{email}}

Tell us what happened, when, and who was involved, as far as you know. You
don't need to be certain something is wrong to raise it. We will treat what you
tell us confidentially, sharing it only when we need to in order to keep
someone safe.

You can also contact these services directly:

- **Concerns about an adult who may be at risk:** your local council's adult social care team.
- **Concerns about a child:** your local council's children's services, or the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000.
- **If you're feeling low or need someone to talk to:** Samaritans, free, any time, on 116 123.

## What we do to keep activities safe

- Every activity has a named organiser responsible for it on the day.
- Hosts and volunteers are briefed on our participation guidelines and on how to raise a concern.
- We check volunteers appropriately for their role, including DBS checks where the law allows and the role requires them.
- We choose public or community venues for activities wherever we can.
- We don't run activities for children or young people unless the right arrangements are in place first.
- We don't publish anyone's name, photograph or story without their clear permission.

Our activities are community activities, not care, health or therapy services.
