Our activities work because people treat each other well. We ask everyone —
participants, hosts, volunteers and partners — to follow these guidelines.

## Be welcoming

Say hello to people you don't know. Make room in the conversation. Remember
that someone may be here for the first time, or the first time in a long time.

## Respect differences

People come from different backgrounds, generations, cultures and beliefs.
Discrimination, harassment and hurtful comments aren't acceptable.

## Respect boundaries

Some people like to chat, others prefer to listen. Don't push anyone to share
more than they want to. Ask before touching, and accept "no" the first time.

## This isn't the place for selling or recruiting

Please don't use activities to sell products, recruit for a cause or a
business, or pursue romantic interest in someone who hasn't shown it's welcome.

## Photos and privacy

Ask before taking photos of other people, and don't share them online without
permission. What people tell you in a group stays in the group, unless someone
is at risk.

## Alcohol and drugs

Please don't attend under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Some activities
may take place where alcohol is sold; nobody should feel pressure to drink.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the activity organiser on the day, or contact us afterwards at {{email}}.
For anything to do with someone's safety, see our [safeguarding page](/safeguarding/).

If someone doesn't follow these guidelines, the organiser may ask them to
leave. Serious or repeated problems may mean we ask someone not to come back.
