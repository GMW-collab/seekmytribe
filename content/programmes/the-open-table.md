summary: >-
  A regular programme of small, hosted group activities — shared meals, walks
  and skill swaps — designed so that someone arriving alone feels welcome from
  the first minute.
problem: >-
  Many people want more contact with others but find open social events
  intimidating, especially when they are new to an area, going through a life
  change, or out of practice. Activities with a shared focus and a host who
  greets newcomers lower that barrier.
participants: >-
  Adults who would like more social contact, with particular attention to people
  new to the area, people living alone, and people returning to social life after
  illness, caring or bereavement. Open to everyone; no referral needed.
activities:
  - A fortnightly shared meal for up to 16 people, cooked together or brought in
  - A weekly short walk at an easy pace, with a café stop
  - A monthly skill swap, where participants teach each other something
  - A trained host at every session whose job is to welcome newcomers
outputs:
  - Number of sessions delivered
  - Number of different people taking part, and how many return
  - Number of volunteer hosts trained
outcomes:
  - Participants report feeling welcome and part of the group
  - Participants report meeting people they would like to see again
  - Some participants go on to host or suggest activities themselves
delivery: >-
  To be developed with the first venue partner. A pilot of roughly three months
  is likely, followed by a review with participants before expanding.
evaluation: >-
  Sign-in records for outputs; a short, optional and anonymous feedback card
  for outcomes; a participant review session at the end of the pilot. See the
  Impact page for definitions.
budget: >-
  Not yet costed. Likely cost lines include venue hire, food, host training,
  DBS checks where needed, insurance, materials and evaluation time.
